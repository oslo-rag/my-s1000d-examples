The Oslo Radar Unit is a compact, high-resolution marine radar system engineered for small vessels, delivering exceptional performance for navigation and collision avoidance in all weather conditions.

Combining a lightweight antenna assembly, integrated transceiver, and high-contrast display unit, it offers professional-grade radar capability in an affordable, easy-to-install package.

12 nautical mile range with crisp target separation

Low-power X-band operation with advanced noise reduction
