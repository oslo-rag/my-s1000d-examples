Replace Oslo Radar Antenna

Preliminary Requirements

Before starting this procedure:

• Power to radar unit is OFF.

Procedure

Remove the four mounting bolts from the antenna base.

Disconnect the coaxial cable connector.

Install the new antenna and torque bolts to 12 Nm.

Reconnect the coaxial cable.

After replacement:

Perform functional test of the radar system.
